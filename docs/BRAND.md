# AproBook: name and philosophy

> Your books, organized and ready — right when you want to read.

**AproBook** brings together three readings of one name:

- **Apro(pos) + Book:** *apropos* means timely or fitting. The app should make the distance between wanting to read and beginning to read as short as possible.
- **A Personal Reading Organizer:** your own books and reading history remain the center of a private, local-first library—not a store or subscription service.
- **A Pro Book:** careful engineering should make the experience feel simple, responsive, and unobtrusive rather than complicated.

Built with Rust through an AI-assisted development workflow, AproBook pairs fast iteration with an emphasis on reliability. The application organizes the library, remembers the reading position, and then gets out of the way.

> AproBook should not demand attention before the book does.

The concrete product description remains: **本地优先的 EPUB、PDF、无 DRM MOBI 与 TXT 书库和阅读器。**
